# Locked Decisions and Remote Configuration

## Locked product decisions

- Four lanes and five cards maximum per lane.
- 90-second standard PvP match.
- Eight-second card placement timer.
- Three equipped powers.
- Server-authoritative ranked play with deterministic seeds.
- Eight launch powers defined in this handoff.
- Twelve existing gameplay directions are theme skins.
- Cosmetic-first monetization with no paid competitive advantage.
- Bottom navigation: Home, Play, Collection, Ranked, Profile.

## Remotely configurable without a client update

- Timers within QA-approved bounds.
- Matchmaking search expansion.
- Power energy cost, cooldown, and duration within a new rules version.
- XP and mission reward amounts.
- Catalog availability and cosmetic prices.
- Event schedules and notification copy.
- Feature flags, maintenance state, minimum client version, graphics defaults.

## Requires a rules-version change

- Score formula.
- Deck or Ace behavior.
- Power mechanical outcome.
- Lane capacity or count.
- Tie-breakers.
- Match terminal conditions.

## Requires explicit product approval before implementation

- Real-money gambling, withdrawable crypto, cash prizes, wagering, entry fees, or purchasable ranked advantages.
- User-generated chat beyond preset quick chat.
- Spectator wagering or prediction markets.
- Loot boxes with paid randomized outcomes.

These areas introduce substantial legal, store-policy, age-gating, payments, fraud, and regional compliance work and are outside this launch handoff.
