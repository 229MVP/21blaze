# Progression, Economy, and Monetization

## Progression

- Player level: 1–50 at launch, then repeatable prestige track.
- XP sources: tutorial, completed matches, wins, exact 21s, missions, daily challenge, first win, and seasonal milestones.
- Ranked rating is separate from player level.
- Powers unlock at fixed levels but have no ranked stat levels.
- Cosmetics have rarity only for collection presentation, not gameplay strength.

## Ranked tiers

Ember I–III, Flame I–III, Inferno I–III, Blaze I–III, and Legendary Blaze. Use hidden MMR for matchmaking and visible rating for progression. First season should last 6 weeks, with a 7-day soft reset window and rewards based on highest achieved tier.

## Currencies

| Currency | Earned | Purchased | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Blaze Coins | Yes | Optional bundles | Standard cosmetics, reroll cosmetic shop, profile items |
| Blaze Gems | Limited | Yes | Premium cosmetics, theme bundles, season pass |
| Ranked Marks | Ranked only | No | Ranked-exclusive cosmetics |
| XP | Gameplay | No direct sale | Player level |

Do not sell Blaze energy, better cards, stronger powers, shorter power cooldowns, extra ranked attempts, or hidden matchmaking advantages.

## Reward cadence

- First win of day.
- Three rotating daily missions.
- Six weekly missions.
- Daily Blaze ranked attempt.
- Player-level rewards.
- Ranked season milestones and end-of-season reward.
- Optional seven-day welcome track for new users.

## Season pass

Free and premium cosmetic tracks. Progress is based on season XP from normal play and missions. Premium rewards may include themes, card backs, power effect skins, victory effects, emotes, profile frames, and currency. No competitive power is exclusive to premium.

## Shop catalog

- Full gameplay themes from the twelve approved directions.
- Card backs and card-face materials.
- Power effect skins.
- Exact-21, bust, and victory effect skins.
- Emotes and quick-chat packs.
- Player frames, titles, banners, and avatars.
- Curated bundles with transparent itemized value.

## Ads

Avoid forced interstitials in PvP. Optional rewarded ads may grant a small amount of Blaze Coins, refresh one cosmetic shop slot, or double a non-ranked daily login reward. Ads must never modify a live or ranked match.

## Economy safeguards

- All grants and spends create append-only ledger entries.
- Server validates balance and ownership transactionally.
- Catalog entries have start/end time, platform availability, price version, and age/region restrictions.
- Purchases support restore, refund/revocation, parental controls, and duplicate transaction protection.
- Remote-config economy changes are versioned and audited.
