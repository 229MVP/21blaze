## Full description

21 Blaze is a fast-paced card strategy game where players race against the clock to build lanes, hit 21, create five-card clears, avoid busts, and chase higher scores.

**Solo timed mode**
- Four lanes, one active card, two minutes on the clock
- Build multiplier streaks with smart clears
- Pause, resume, or restart anytime

**Progress and style**
- Track local high scores and compete on the global verified leaderboard when online
- Earn Blaze Coins and unlock cosmetics in Blaze Locker
- Claim daily login rewards and missions in Blaze Rewards

**Built for mobile**
- Sound, haptics, and Reduced Motion settings
- How to Play reference built into the app

21 Blaze focuses on skill, speed, and lane strategy. No account is required to play Solo.

_Note: In-app purchases are not available in this release._
