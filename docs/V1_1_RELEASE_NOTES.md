# 21 Blaze — Version 1.1 Release Notes

**Version 1.1.0** — Blaze Rewards, Blaze Locker, and retention polish.

## What's new

- **Blaze Coins** — earn coins by playing Solo matches, with a bonus for
  your first match of the day and for active play time.
- **Daily Missions** — three fresh missions every day, each with its own
  Blaze Coin (and sometimes XP) reward.
- **Daily Streak** — build a streak for escalating daily rewards, capped
  off by an exclusive "Seven Day Blaze" title on Day 7.
- **Blaze Locker** — spend your Blaze Coins on code-driven cosmetics: an
  Ember Card Back, Gold Lane Glow, Midnight Card Style, Flame Profile
  Frame, and Lava Arena — all earnable, none paid.
- **Earnable cosmetics** — every cosmetic in this release is unlocked
  with Blaze Coins you earn by playing, or through the daily streak.
  Nothing in this update requires a purchase.
- **Performance and stability improvements** — centralized ad handling,
  a corrected personalized-ads/privacy safeguard on iOS, and general
  polish across the daily rewards, missions, and locker screens.

## What did not change

- Scoring, timers, card order, and match rules are unchanged.
- No new paid products, subscriptions, loot boxes, or paid currency were
  added.
- No multiplayer modes, tournaments, or clans were added.

---

*This release does not enable any purchase or subscription flow. All new
rewards and cosmetics are earned through gameplay.*
